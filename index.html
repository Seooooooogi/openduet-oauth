OpenDuet (personal assistant)

OpenDuet is a self-hosted personal assistant that one person runs on their own computer. It is not a public service and it is not offered to other users.

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar to summarize schedules, find missed messages and draft replies. It sends a reply only when the owner asks for it.
