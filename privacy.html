OpenDuet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Who uses this app

OpenDuet is a personal, self-hosted assistant. Its only user is its owner, who also operates it. No other person's account is connected.

Google data it accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): searches and reads the owner's messages to summarize them and find missed items.
  • Gmail (gmail.send): sends a reply only when the owner asks for it.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly): reads the owner's events.

How the data is processed and where it goes

  • OpenDuet runs on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in its local database.
  • To answer a request, the relevant message or event content is sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic, Claude API).
  • Conversation threads are stored by CopilotKit Intelligence, the conversation service that OpenDuet uses.
  • The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Limited Use

OpenDuet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting Google in OpenDuet deletes the stored token.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

개인정보처리방침 (요약)

  • 사용자: 운영자 본인 1명뿐입니다.
  • 접근 데이터: Gmail 읽기, 요청할 때만 Gmail 답장 보내기, Google Calendar 읽기.
  • 처리: 운영자 컴퓨터에서 실행합니다. 답변을 만들 때 관련 내용을 Anthropic(Claude API)에 보내고, 대화 기록은 CopilotKit Intelligence 에 저장합니다.
  • 판매·광고·제3자 공유: 하지 않습니다.
  • 삭제: OpenDuet 에서 연결을 해제하거나 Google 계정의 권한 페이지에서 접근을 취소하면 됩니다.
